About

I'm Jerry, a hydrogeology postgraduate. This site does two things: it hosts my essays, and it hosts the Library — a reference index for the profession. The goal: when a question comes up, search here and find out in seconds which book, and which chapter, to open.

How the Library works

The Library is a chapter-level index, not a full-text collection. Each book is catalogued with its bibliographic record, topics, a summary, "best for" guidance, the full text of its contents pages, and its physical location. Find the entry, then go read the chapter. The index thickens as I study — chapter summaries, keywords, page numbers and reading notes get attached over time.

A caveat

Many of the catalogued volumes date from 1970–1995. The classic theory and methods hold up; anything touching current regulation, standards, equipment or safety practice does not. Every entry carries a currency-risk rating and a pointer to the modern source to consult instead.
