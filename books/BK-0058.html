Physical and Chemical Hydrogeology

Covers the origin of porosity and permeability, groundwater movement, flow equations, aquifer testing, resource evaluation, regional flow, crustal fluids and heat transport, with chemical-hydrogeology principles integrated throughout and developed in the later chapters.

Best for: hydrogeologists and postgraduates needing a rigorous, integrated first-edition treatment of physical and chemical groundwater principles, especially flow, testing and regional systems

Reading context

The core physical principles—Darcy's law, storage equations, aquifer testing and regional flow—remain sound, but the contaminant-transport, modelling and reaction chapters were substantially revised in the 1998 second edition (BK-0041). Do not rely on this for current regulatory guidance, numerical modelling practices or up-to-date contaminant hydrogeology.

Use the revised second edition (Domenico & Schwartz, Wiley, 1998, ISBN 978-0-471-59762-9) for updated contaminant transport, modelling and reaction chapters; no publisher-authorised open full text exists for either edition.
