Environmental Control and Public Health — Technology: a second level and post-experience course (PT272) - set of five course units: Units 1-2, Units 11-12, Units 13-14, Unit 15 and Unit 16

This five-unit Open University set introduces pollution and environmental health through the commons, biosphere, biogeochemical cycles, pollutant classification and effects, then covers control of the acoustic environment, air pollution and its control, and planning control.

Best for: historical study of 1970s UK environmental health teaching and early interdisciplinary pollution science

Reading context

The foundational thermodynamic and biogeochemical cycle explanations remain valid as introductory science, but the legal, policy, standards, pollution data, case studies and control technologies are entirely superseded. Do not rely on this for current UK environmental regulation, air or noise standards, exposure limits, or modern pollution-control engineering.

No later edition of this unit set; use the Open University's successor module T210 Environmental control and public health (2004, digitally borrowable on the Internet Archive) and the OpenLearn course 'Integrated safety, health and environmental management: an introduction' as modern replacements.
