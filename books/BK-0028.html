Groundwater Pollution, Aquifer Recharge and Vulnerability

Edited collection of 19 papers examining aquifer recharge, pollution vulnerability and groundwater source protection, with UK and Irish case studies plus international examples from Jersey, Nebraska, Mexico and Yemen, and methods for vulnerability mapping and source protection zone delineation.

Best for: UK and Irish groundwater vulnerability mapping, recharge estimation and source protection zone methods, with international comparative case studies

Reading context

The recharge and vulnerability concepts, field techniques and many case-study descriptions remain valid as historical and methodological references. Do not rely on this for current UK groundwater protection policy, source protection zone delineation or vulnerability maps; use the Environment Agency's current groundwater protection position statements, 2017 vulnerability mapping methodology and 2019 SPZ manual.

No later edition; the volume is available online through the Lyell Collection (subscription), while current UK practice is best served by the Environment Agency's groundwater protection position statements, 2017 vulnerability mapping methodology and 2019 SPZ manual.
