Introduction to Physical Hydrology

Organised around the water cycle and catchment systems: the global water cycle, precipitation and evapotranspiration, soil water, infiltration, overland flow, groundwater, open channels, snow and ice, flood hydrographs and river regimes.

Best for: understanding catchment-systems thinking, disciplinary history and the links between hydrological processes and landforms
