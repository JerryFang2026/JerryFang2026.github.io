Hydraulic Fracturing to Improve the Yield of Bores in Fractured Rock

Describes hydraulic fracturing operations to enhance bore yields in fractured rock. Includes equipment details, site selection, pressure data, and pumping test summaries from investigation sites.

Best for: Operational details and test data from hydraulic fracturing field trials.
