A Guide to the Selection of Materials for Monitoring Well Construction and Ground-Water Sampling

Evaluates material compatibility for monitoring well construction and groundwater sampling. Covers casing, pumps, grouts, and sampling devices, with tables on chemical exposure and material compatibility under various groundwater conditions.

Best for: Selecting well casing, sampling devices, and materials compatible with specific groundwater chemistries.
