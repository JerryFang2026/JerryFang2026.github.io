Igneous and Metamorphic Petrology

A 1951 textbook covering principles of chemical equilibrium applied to rocks, volcanic and plutonic associations, and the fabric of deformed rocks. Includes petrofabric analysis and classification of igneous and metamorphic systems.

Best for: Rock classification, chemical equilibrium, plutonic associations, and petrofabric analysis of deformed rocks.

Reading context

A classic petrology text predating much modern plate-tectonic and geochemical interpretation. Use it alongside a later geology reference.
