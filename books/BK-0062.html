Aquifer-Test Design, Observation and Data Analysis

USGS manual on aquifer-test design, observation and data analysis: develops the working hypothesis, reviews type curves, covers test design (site evaluation, response prediction, field observation of water levels and discharge), and data analysis by drawdown corrections and type-curve matching, including unconfined aquifers.

Best for: the original USGS type-curve approach to designing aquifer tests and hand-matching drawdown/recovery data to type curves, including unconfined aquifers

Reading context

The type-curve theory and test-design logic remain valid, but routine practice now relies on software such as USGS spreadsheets or AQTESOLV and on later USGS/ASTM guidance rather than hand-matched curves. Do not rely on this for modern data-acquisition, instrumentation, or regulatory standards.

No later edition; the full report is freely available from the USGS (https://pubs.usgs.gov/twri/twri3-b1/pdf/twri_3-B1_a.pdf), and Halford & Kuniansky (2002, USGS OFR 02-197) plus Kruseman & de Ridder (2000) are the authoritative modern companions for spreadsheet-based analysis.
