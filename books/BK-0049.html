Geographical Approaches to Fluvial Processes

An edited collection of fourteen field-based studies covering chemical weathering and solution processes, runoff and erosion in small catchments, and channel sediments and morphology, with British, Malaysian, Australian and Luxembourg case studies.

Reading context

The tripartite division of chemical weathering, hydrophysical processes and channel morphology remains a clear organisational frame, but the field methods, statistical analyses and process interpretations predate modern digital terrain analysis, sensor-based monitoring and river-restoration practice; do not rely on this for current hydrological modelling, sediment transport formulae, channel classification or regulatory guidance.

No later edition exists; the best modern companion is Kondolf & Piégay (eds) (2016) Tools in Fluvial Geomorphology, 2nd edn, Wiley, with the open Tricart (1981) review available at Persée for contemporary assessment.
