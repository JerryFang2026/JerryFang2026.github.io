Guidelines for the Selection of Turbine Pump Materials for Use in Groundwaters

Presents corrosion-based guidance for selecting turbine pump materials in Australian groundwaters, covering water corrosivity indicators, recommendations for bowls, impellers and column pipe, alternative materials, and field methods for pH, dissolved oxygen, Langelier index and Cl/CO3 ratios.

Best for: historical groundwater corrosivity assessment and turbine-pump material selection using Langelier index and Cl/CO3 ratios

Reading context

The general corrosivity reasoning (pH, dissolved oxygen, Langelier index, chloride/carbonate ratios) remains instructive, but the recommended metal grades and material choices pre-date modern stainless steels, polymer/composite components and the current Australian bore-construction standard. Do not rely on this for current material specifications or regulatory compliance in water bore construction.

No later edition; use the Minimum Construction Requirements for Water Bores in Australia (4th ed., 2020) for current casing, screen and pump material selection, with Houben & Treskatis (2007) for corrosion and rehabilitation.
