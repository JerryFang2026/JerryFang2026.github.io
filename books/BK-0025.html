Hydrogeology in the Service of Man — Part 2 — Economic and Social Influence

The influence of groundwater on agriculture, industry, cities, drought resilience and social development, with numerous 1980s case studies from Europe, Africa, Asia, the Americas and beyond.

Best for: finding global case studies, establishing historical baselines, and comparing groundwater development with its socio-economic impacts
