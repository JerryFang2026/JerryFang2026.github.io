Hydrogeology of Hard Rocks — Part 1 — Memoires of the XXIVth Congress International Association of Hydrogeologists 28th June - 2nd July 1993, Ås (Oslo), Norway.

Part I covers rock fracturing, recharge and flow, chemical and isotopic groundwater composition, and locating fractured aquifers.

Best for: Locating papers on fractured reservoirs, recharge, hydrochemistry and aquifer exploration.
