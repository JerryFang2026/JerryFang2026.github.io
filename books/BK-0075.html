The Detection of Boundaries in Leaky Aquifers

Addresses the detection of hydraulic boundaries in leaky aquifers during constant-rate pumping tests, covering fault hydrogeology, radial flow and image-well theory, identification of single boundaries, and practical constraints such as instrumentation, pumping rate, geometry, time, natural fluctuations and data accuracy.

Best for: understanding the theory and practical limitations of detecting boundaries in leaky aquifers from constant-rate pumping tests

Reading context

The analytical treatment of image-well theory and leaky-aquifer responses remains valid, but the report predates routine drawdown-derivative diagnostics, numerical Laplace inversion and modern pumping-test software. Do not rely on this for current regulatory guidance or automated interpretation; use Kruseman & de Ridder (1994) or later for practical analysis.

No later edition of this report; use Kruseman & de Ridder, Analysis and Evaluation of Pumping Test Data, 2nd ed. (1994), available open access via The Groundwater Project, as the authoritative modern replacement.
