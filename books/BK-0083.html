Principles of Engineering Economy

A textbook on engineering economy covering basic concepts, judging investment attractiveness, and techniques for economy studies. Includes analysis of cash flow, compound interest tables, and notes on income tax laws.

Best for: Economic analysis methods, investment evaluation, and cash flow techniques.
