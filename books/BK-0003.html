Soils Contaminated by Petroleum — Environmental and Public Health Effects

An early interdisciplinary collection on petroleum-contaminated soils covering transport modelling, vadose-zone vapour detection, human exposure, public health, microbial degradation, soil vapour extraction, land treatment and underground storage-tank regulation.

Best for: understanding petroleum-hydrocarbon contaminated-site terminology, the origins of technical approaches and early case studies

Reading context

Analytical methods, exposure parameters, clean-up thresholds, remediation processes and regulation have been substantially updated; do not adopt design values or standards directly from this book.

Real-world projects should be re-evaluated against a current site conceptual model, risk assessment and verification procedures.
