Flow in Channels

An introductory civil engineering hydraulics text on open-channel flow covering uniform, gradually varied and rapidly varied flow, erodible channels, and the fundamental principles of energy, momentum and resistance, with a bibliography.

Best for: a concise historical introduction to open-channel flow fundamentals and erodible-channel concepts

Reading context

The basic physics of uniform, gradually varied and rapidly varied flow and qualitative erodible-channel behaviour remain valid. Do not rely on this for computational methods, design standards, or modern numerical modelling; consult Chadwick et al. (2021) or current national codes.

No later edition; use Chadwick, Morfett & Borthwick, Hydraulics in Civil and Environmental Engineering (6th ed., 2021) for modern methods, and the Springer digitised eBook (DOI 10.1007/978-1-349-00195-8) for the original text.
