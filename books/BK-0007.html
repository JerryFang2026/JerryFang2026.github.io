Groundwater Hydrology

An engineering-oriented general groundwater reference covering groundwater occurrence, well hydraulics, water wells, water-level responses, quality and pollution, management, models, artificial recharge and seawater intrusion.

Best for: quickly looking up traditional formulae, design methods, well hydraulics and resource-management frameworks by engineering problem

Reading context
