A Guide to Water Well Casing and Screen Selection

A manufacturer's guide covering well construction methods, casing and screen materials, manufacturing processes, and end-use comparisons. Includes general considerations and casing requirements for water wells.

Best for: Selecting casing and screen materials based on manufacturing processes and well construction methods.
