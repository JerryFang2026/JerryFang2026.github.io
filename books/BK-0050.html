BK-0050 · THE G15 BOOKSHELF

Processing and Synthesis of Hydrogeological Data

Covers statistical analysis of permeable lithofacies and trend surfaces, hydrodynamic interpretation of pumping tests in steady and unsteady flow, petrophysical controls on sand drainage, and correlation of natural factors with aquifer potential, using Romanian case studies.

Best for: historical review of pre-computer statistical and graphical methods for lithofacies mapping, pumping-test interpretation, and aquifer characterisation

Reading context

The basic analytical solutions for confined, unconfined, leaky and recovery tests remain mathematically valid, and the statistical concepts such as trend surfaces and entropy are still recognisable. However, the manual's hand and graphical workflows predate desktop computing, GIS, geostatistics and numerical modelling, and must not be used for current data synthesis or regulatory reporting. Do not rely on this for modern pumping-test software or data-analysis standards.

No later English edition; the Internet Archive holds a borrowable digitised copy, and Kresic & Mikszewski (2013) Hydrogeological Conceptual Site Models: Data Analysis and Visualization is the modern replacement.
