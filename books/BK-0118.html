Practical Guide for Ground-Water Sampling

A technical report detailing procedures for groundwater sampling. Covers well development, purging strategies, sampling mechanisms, and potential sources of error during sample collection and analysis.

Best for: Protocols for purging, selecting sampling mechanisms, and understanding error sources in groundwater monitoring.
