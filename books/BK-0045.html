Groundwater in the Celtic Regions — Studies in Hard Rock and Quaternary Hydrogeology

An edited Geological Society Special Publication presenting case studies on groundwater in hard-rock and Quaternary aquifers of Ireland, Scotland, Wales, Cornwall and Cape Breton, covering protection and karst management, resource evaluation, data-scarce aquifer modelling, geophysics, and island supply.

Best for: regional hard-rock and Quaternary aquifer case studies across Ireland, Scotland, Wales and the Celtic fringe, including protection, karst, data-scarce aquifer evaluation, geophysics and island supplies

Reading context

The hydrogeological descriptions and many local case studies remain valid, but the regulatory framework predates the Water Framework Directive, groundwater-body status reporting, and modern modelling and vulnerability-mapping practice. Do not rely on this for current groundwater quality thresholds, WFD status classifications, or recent abstraction and climate-change projections; consult the later national syntheses.

No later edition; the Lyell Collection provides subscription access to all chapters, and the best open modern companions are BGS Earthwise's Hydrogeology of Wales and MacDonald et al. (2005) for Scotland.
