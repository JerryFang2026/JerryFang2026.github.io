Physical Geology

Introductory geology textbook covering minerals, weathering, soil, geological time, and groundwater. Visible groundwater topics include the water table, groundwater movement, springs, aquifers, wells, and groundwater pollution.

Best for: Basic physical geology and introductory groundwater concepts.
