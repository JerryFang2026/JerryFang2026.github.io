Impacts of Urban Growth on Surface Water and Groundwater Quality

Proceedings examining how urban expansion affects water quality. Includes case studies from Mombasa, arid northwest China, and the Niger Delta, addressing resource utilization, eco-environmental dynamics, and water quality changes.
