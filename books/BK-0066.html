Study and Modelling of Saltwater Intrusion into Aquifers — Proceedings of the Saltwater Intrusion Meeting, 1-6 November 1992, Barcelona, Spain (12th Salt Water Intrusion Meeting, SWIM 12)

Proceedings of the 12th Salt Water Intrusion Meeting (Barcelona, 1992) covering general and chemical aspects of saltwater intrusion, sea-level rise and coastal aquifer salinisation, with survey, monitoring and control case studies from Mediterranean, Belgian and Dutch coastal, Chinese and Pacific island settings.

Best for: documented early-1990s saltwater-intrusion field cases and monitoring approaches from Mediterranean and North Sea coastal aquifers, plus contemporaneous variable-density modelling

Reading context

The hydrochemical and field-monitoring descriptions for the Llobregat delta, Belgian and Dutch coasts and Spanish island aquifers remain useful reference cases, but the variable-density modelling methods are superseded by SEAWAT, SUTRA and FEFLOW. Do not rely on this for current regulatory, water-quality or sea-level-rise adaptation guidance; check against modern national and EU frameworks.

No later edition exists; all papers are open access on the SWIM official site (swim-site.nl), with Bear et al. (1999) and Werner et al. (2013) as the authoritative modern companion texts.
