Manual of Water Well Construction Practices

EPA manual detailing contractual and technical specifications for water well construction. Contents include general conditions, insurance, formation and aquifer sampling, well construction methods, sanitary protection, casing seating, grout pressure testing, and screen selection.

Best for: Contract specifications, construction standards, and sampling procedures for water supply wells.
