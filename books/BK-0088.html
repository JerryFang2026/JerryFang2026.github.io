Calibration and Reliability in Groundwater Modelling — Coping with Uncertainty

Proceedings from an IAHS conference addressing uncertainty in groundwater modelling. Sampled papers cover fracture network transport, karst and dual porosity modelling, parameter estimation using genetic algorithms, inverse modelling, model calibration, and reactive transport of contaminants like DNAPLs.

Best for: Conference papers on model calibration, uncertainty analysis, and parameter estimation techniques.
