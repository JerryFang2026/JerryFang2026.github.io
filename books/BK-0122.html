Sobreexplotacion de Acuiferos / Aquifer Overexploitation — A.I.H. - XXIII Congreso Internacional / I.A.H. - XXIII International Congress. Actas / Proceedings. Tomo I: Ponencias, Comunicaciones y Resúmenes de Posters / Volume I: General Papers, Extended Abstracts and Posters

Proceedings of the 1991 IAH International Congress on aquifer overexploitation. Topics include hydrogeological and hydrochemical characterization, environmental effects, protective measures, legal and socioeconomic problems, and case histories from developing countries and Europe.

Best for: Aquifer overexploitation case studies, management measures, and legal aspects.
