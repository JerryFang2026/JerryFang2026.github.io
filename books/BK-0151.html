Understanding Hydraulics

A textbook covering fundamental hydraulics principles. Chapters address hydrostatics, fluid motion, flow measurement, pipelines, open channels, hydraulic structures like dams and weirs, and applications of engineering hydrology.

Best for: Hydraulic principles, flow equations, and design of hydraulic structures.
