The Rivers Handbook — Hydrological and Ecological Principles. Volume 2

This edited volume covers river perturbations and biological impacts, monitoring programmes, hydrological and water-quality modelling, management options from flow allocation to restoration, and regional case studies in North America, Iceland and dryland rivers.

Reading context

The core hydrological and ecological principles remain useful background; however, chapters on water-quality standards, flow allocation, river engineering and restoration are pre-Water Framework Directive and two decades of restoration practice. Do not rely on this for current EU water-quality regulation, modern environmental flow methods, or restoration design guidance.

No later edition; the 2009 Wiley Online Library version is a re-issue of the 1994 text, and Allan, Castillo & Capps, Stream Ecology (3rd edn, 2021) is the authoritative modern replacement for the ecological principles.
