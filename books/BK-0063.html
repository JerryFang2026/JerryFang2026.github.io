Introduction to Ground-Water Hydraulics — A Programed Text for Self-Instruction

A programmed self-instruction text covering definitions and general concepts, Darcy's law and field applications, groundwater drainage, unidirectional and radial non-equilibrium flow, finite-difference methods, analog techniques, and the groundwater-flow equation.

Best for: self-paced grounding in classical groundwater hydraulics, from Darcy's law through finite-difference methods

Reading context

The derivations of Darcy's law, storage, transient flow, well hydraulics, and finite-difference methods remain mathematically sound and continue to be used in USGS training. The chapter on electric-analog techniques is obsolete and the programmed format is dated; do not rely on this for modern numerical modelling, software workflows, or current regulatory guidance.

No later edition of this programmed text exists; use the open official USGS PDF (TWRI 3-B2) and the companion TWRI 3-B5 and 3-B6 chapters for boundary conditions and superposition, with Heath 1983 as a modern primer.
