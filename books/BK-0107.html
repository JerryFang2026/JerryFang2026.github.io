A Formula for Computing Transmissibility Causing Maximum Possible Drawdown Due to Pumping

Presents a formula for calculating transmissibility related to maximum drawdown from pumping. Includes development, illustration, and summary of the formula within a groundwater hydraulics context.

Best for: Looking up the specific formula and its derivation for pumping-induced drawdown.
