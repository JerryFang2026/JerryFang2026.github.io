Groundwater: Modelling, Management and Contamination

This edited research volume compiles 14 chapters on groundwater modelling and contamination, covering fundamentals, GIS-based aquifer modelling, neural network prediction, air sparging design, surface-water interactions, water-solid interactions, subsurface contaminants, arsenic treatment wetlands, and coastal groundwater management.

Best for: specialised case studies on GIS-based aquifer modelling, air sparging design, surface-water interactions and coastal groundwater management; not a systematic textbook

Reading context

The basic conceptual principles of groundwater flow and transport modelling remain valid, but the modelling examples and parameter estimation approaches predate current calibration and uncertainty workflows and modern groundwater modelling codes. Do not rely on this for current contaminant fate guidance, regulatory screening values, or accepted numerical modelling practice.

No later edition; for current groundwater modelling practice use Anderson, Woessner & Hunt (2015) Applied Groundwater Modeling, 2nd ed., and for contaminant-specific remediation topics consult current regulatory guidance.
