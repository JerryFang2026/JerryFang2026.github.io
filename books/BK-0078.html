Pumping Tests — A guide to the testing of water wells for public, industrial, and farm supplies

A concise 26-page Irish national guidance circular on pumping test practice for water wells, covering planning, measurement of pumping rate, water level and time, drawdown and specific capacity, recommended preliminary, step and constant-rate test programmes, and interpretation of results.

Best for: a concise field procedure for single-well pumping tests: pump setting, metering, water-level timing, discharge and step/constant-rate programmes

Reading context

The basic drawdown-analysis concepts remain valid, and the field-measurement checklist for pumping rate, water level and time is still sound. Do not rely on this for current Irish regulatory requirements, test design and reporting under ISO 14686:2003 and IGI Water Well Guidelines (2007), or modern software-based analysis methods.

No later edition; use ISO 14686:2003 for design and IGI (2007)/EPA Advice Note 14 for Irish regulatory practice, with Kruseman & de Ridder (2nd ed., 1994, open access) for analysis theory.
