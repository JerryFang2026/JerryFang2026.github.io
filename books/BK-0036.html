Broad monograph on geothermal resources covering the origins of Earth heat, geothermal systems and models, exploration, resource assessment, exploitation, environmental impact, and uses of geothermal energy, with case studies from the USA, Kenya, New Zealand, Europe and the UK.

Reading context

The origins of Earth heat and basic conductive/convective geothermal system models still hold, but the exploration, resource assessment and exploitation chapters predate enhanced/engineered geothermal systems, ground-source heat pumps, modern reservoir simulation and the current UK deep-geothermal programme. Do not rely on this for current resource-assessment methods, EGS design, or UK deep-geothermal project data.

No later edition; the 1989 second edition is available as a Springer reissue, but modern practice is better served by Grant & Bixley (2011) Geothermal Reservoir Engineering, 2nd ed. and Glassley (2014) Geothermal Energy: Renewable Energy and the Environment, 2nd ed.
