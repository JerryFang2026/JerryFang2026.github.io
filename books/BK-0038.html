Urban Drainage

From rainfall and runoff to sewer hydraulics, water quality, structural design, operation, rehabilitation, flow and quality modelling, stormwater management, and low-income community sanitation, this textbook covers the planning, design, and management of urban drainage systems.

Reading context

Core hydrology, hydraulics and water-quality principles remain sound, but design methods, CSO control, modelling packages and regulatory references predate SuDS practice, the Flood and Water Management Act 2010 and current UK sewer design/adoption guidance. Do not rely on this for current UK design standards, sustainable drainage design, or software selection.

Use Urban Drainage, 5th edition by Butler, Digman, Makropoulos & Davies (CRC Press, 2024) as the current replacement; it supersedes this first edition for practice.
