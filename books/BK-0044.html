The Field Guide to Water Wells and Boreholes

Covers the design, materials and construction of water wells and boreholes, including hand-dug wells, drilling methods, formation and groundwater sampling, geophysical and lithological logging, well development, pumping tests, and maintenance, with design tables and conversion appendices.

Best for: field hydrogeologists and engineers needing a compact guide to well design, construction, sampling, logging, development, testing and maintenance

Reading context

The basic field logging, sampling and well-development concepts remain sound as a historical introduction, but drilling technology, casing and screen materials, geophysical logging, and well-test analysis have moved on considerably. Do not rely on this for current UK regulatory requirements, modern drilling methods, or up-to-date water-quality sampling protocols.

The successor work by Misstear, Banks & Clark, Water Wells and Boreholes (Wiley 2006; 2nd ed. 2017) is the authoritative modern replacement and should be used instead.
