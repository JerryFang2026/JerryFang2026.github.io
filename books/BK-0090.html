Improvement of Methods of Long Term Prediction of Variations in Groundwater Resources and Regimes Due to Human Activity

Proceedings from an IAHS symposium on predicting groundwater resource changes from human activity. Sampled papers discuss regional forecasting for inter-basin transfers, artificial recharge, land use effects on recharge, parameter sensitivity analysis, and management simulation models for specific basins.

Best for: Methodologies for forecasting groundwater resources and regimes under human-induced stresses.

Reading context

These 1982 proceedings record historical approaches to human impacts on groundwater. They do not describe present-day resources or current forecasting practice.
