Hydrogeology Field Manual

Covers hydrogeologic field investigations from site selection, note-taking, geology and aquifer properties, shallow geophysics, groundwater flow and surface-water interaction, water-chemistry sampling, pumping-test design, and analytical aquifer hydraulics including well water-level measurement and traditional pumping-test interpretation.

Best for: field hydrogeologists needing practical procedures for groundwater monitoring, surface-water exchange measurements, water-chemistry sampling, and pumping-test design and analysis

Reading context

Most field procedures for water-level measurement, stream gauging, seepage meters, and traditional pumping-test analysis remain valid. Do not rely on this for current regulatory sampling and data-quality requirements, karst hydrogeology, or modern logging and telemetry; the third edition revises these.

Use the thoroughly revised third edition, Practical Hydrogeology: Principles and Field Applications (Weight, McGraw Hill, 2019, ISBN 978-1-260-11689-2), which updates sampling, karst, and regulatory content; no open-access full text is available.
