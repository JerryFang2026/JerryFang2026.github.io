Reading context

The hydraulics and water-balance chapters remain sound for basic principles, but the modelling chapter describes analogue and early digital methods and must be replaced by modern numerical groundwater modelling texts. Do not rely on this for contaminant transport, regulatory frameworks, or current standards; consult the 1986 2nd edition or modern replacements.

Use the 1986 2nd edition (Groundwater) for a revised but still dated treatment; for modern open-access fundamentals see Freeze & Cherry (1979) or Woessner & Poeter (2020), and for an authoritative current textbook use Fetter & Kreamer's Applied Hydrogeology (5th ed.).
