Writing a Dissertation For Dummies

A guide to planning, structuring, and writing a dissertation. Visible contents cover research questions, time management, ethics, reading, note-taking, and empirical data collection methods such as surveys, interviews, observations, and case studies.

Best for: Dissertation structure, research planning, and data collection methods.
