Introduction to Geochemistry

A systematic treatment of geochemical principles from aqueous solution equilibria, solution-mineral reactions, crystal and surface chemistry, thermodynamics, redox and isotopes to weathering, soils, sedimentation, diagenesis, and the fluid envelopes, with worked phase diagrams and appendices of crustal abundances.

Best for: students and professionals needing a systematic introduction to aqueous geochemistry, thermodynamics, redox, isotopes and rock-water interaction

Reading context

The equilibrium thermodynamics, aqueous speciation, redox, and stable/radiogenic isotope framework remains valid and pedagogically sound. The thermodynamic data, environmental geochemistry chapter, and associated numerical-modelling practice are 30 years old; do not rely on this for current thermodynamic databases, regulatory limits, or modern geochemical software workflows.

No later edition; for an authoritative modern general geochemistry replacement use White (2020) Geochemistry, 2nd ed., and for groundwater-focused hydrogeochemistry use Appelo & Postma (2005) Geochemistry, Groundwater and Pollution, 2nd ed., ideally with PHREEQC.
