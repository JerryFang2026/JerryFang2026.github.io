Fundamentals of Ground Water

Covers classical well hydraulics (Thiem, Theis, Cooper-Jacob, Hvorslev, Bouwer-Rice) for confined, leaky confined, and unconfined aquifers, plus slug, step, and recovery tests, superposition with image wells, and advective-dispersive mass transport.

Best for: classical analytical aquifer-test methods (Theis, Cooper-Jacob, Hvorslev, Bouwer-Rice) and introductory advective-dispersive transport

Reading context

The analytical derivations for aquifer-test interpretation and advective-dispersive transport remain valid and are carried into the 2nd edition. Do not rely on this 2003 edition for current regulatory frameworks, sustainability assessments, or modern software-based practice.

Use the 2nd edition, Fundamentals of Groundwater (Schwartz & Zhang, Wiley, 2024, ISBN 978-1-119-82013-0) as the current replacement.
