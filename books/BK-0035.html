Groundwater Hydrology — Conceptual and Computational Models

Introduces conceptual and computational groundwater modelling from first principles, covering three-dimensional flow, recharge estimation, surface-water interaction, numerical methods, saline intrusion and quality issues, with UK and international case studies throughout.

Best for: groundwater specialists building conceptual models before numerical simulation, especially for recharge and surface-water interaction

Reading context

The conceptual-model philosophy and the UK/India case studies remain relevant and still cited, but the numerical methods and software context are dated. Do not rely on this for current modelling software practice or computational implementation; check numerical schemes and standards against Anderson, Woessner & Hunt (2015).

No later edition; for current numerical groundwater modelling, use Anderson, Woessner & Hunt (2015) Applied Groundwater Modeling: Simulation of Flow and Advective Transport, 2nd ed.
