Physical and Chemical Hydrogeology

Reading context

The core flow and mass-transport theory, Darcy's law derivations, aquifer-test analysis, and basin hydrology remain sound and widely cited. However, numerical-modelling methods, remediation technologies, and risk-assessment frameworks have advanced significantly; do not rely on this for current regulatory guidance or software implementation.

No later edition; use Schwartz & Zhang, Fundamentals of Groundwater, 2nd edition (Wiley, 2024) as the authoritative modern replacement.
