Natural Inorganic Hydrochemistry in Relation to Groundwater — An Introduction

Runs from groundwater inorganic chemistry, sampling and measurement, graphical presentation of data, saline waters, environmental isotopes and rock-water reactions to corrosion and encrustation, with emphasis on turning chemical results into hydrogeological interpretation.

Best for: major ions, Piper diagrams, mixing, ion exchange, redox, carbonate equilibria and interpreting salinisation
