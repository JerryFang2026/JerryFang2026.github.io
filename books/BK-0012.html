Reading context

Do not use this to select PPE, safety distances, reporting thresholds, monitoring methods or disposal routes; its content predates modern HAZWOPER, ICS and a large body of current regulation.

Field incidents should be handled by trained personnel using the latest SDS, current regulations and the Emergency Response Guidebook.
