The Properties of Groundwater

A translation of Matthess's German textbook on groundwater quality, presenting the physical and chemical properties of groundwater, including the occurrence of dissolved chemical constituents such as minor constituents and trace elements.

Best for: classic hydrochemical principles and natural-water composition data, especially trace-element occurrence

Reading context

The treatment of natural groundwater composition and hydrochemical principles remains conceptually sound, but analytical methods, isotope and dating techniques, geochemical modelling (e.g. PHREEQC), and contaminant hydrogeology are dated. Do not rely on this for regulatory limits, current sampling protocols, or modern geochemical software.

No later English edition exists; the German original was revised as a third edition in 1994 (ISBN 978-3-443-01008-9), and the best modern companion or replacement is Appelo & Postma, Geochemistry, Groundwater and Pollution, 2nd ed. (2005).
