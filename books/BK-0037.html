Geothermal Energy — The Potential in the United Kingdom

This BGS report assesses the United Kingdom's geothermal potential through chapters on heat flow, hot dry rock granites, Mesozoic and Devonian/Carboniferous sedimentary basins, engineering and economic aspects, and a national review with maps and data appendices.

Reading context

The heat-flow and geological framework data remain valuable baseline information, but resource figures, technology assumptions and policy context are dated. Do not rely on this for current deep-geothermal or mine-water resource estimates; check against Busby (2010) and Busby & Terrington (2017).

No later edition; the BGS open-access PDF (NORA WZ86003.pdf) is the official full text, and Busby & Terrington (2017) provides the authoritative modern resource assessment.
