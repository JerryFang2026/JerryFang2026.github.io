Lake and Reservoir Restoration Guidance Manual

EPA manual on lake and reservoir restoration. Visible sections address lake ecology, eutrophication, problem identification, water quality prediction models, point and nonpoint source improvement projects, and restoration techniques including fish management and acidic lakes.

Best for: Lake restoration techniques, eutrophication management, and water quality prediction.
