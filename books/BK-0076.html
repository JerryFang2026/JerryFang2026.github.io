The age of groundwaters in the Chalk and Pleistocene sands of north-east Suffolk

Presents tritium, carbon-14, stable-isotope and basic chemical data for groundwaters in the Chalk and Pleistocene sands of north-east Suffolk; interprets apparent ages and appraises the effect of matrix diffusion on carbon-14 age estimates.

Best for: historical isotope-age estimates, baseline chemistry and matrix-diffusion correction for the Chalk and Crag of north-east Suffolk

Reading context

The raw isotope and basic chemical data remain a valid historical baseline for the Chalk–Crag system, but the derived residence times and salinity interpretations have been revised by EA/BGS Baseline Series reports (e.g. Ander et al. 2006) and 2024 QJEGH syntheses. Do not rely on this for modern age dating using CFC/SF6, 3H/3He or 4He, or for current conceptual models of flow and saline intrusion.

No later edition; use Ander, Shand & Wood (2006) Baseline Report Series 21: The Chalk and Crag of north Norfolk and the Waveney catchment (BGS CR/06/043N; open PDF on NORA) as the authoritative modern replacement.
