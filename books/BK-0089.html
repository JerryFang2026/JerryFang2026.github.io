Mathematical Modeling of Groundwater Pollution

A textbook on the mathematical and numerical modelling of groundwater contamination. Covers hydrodynamic dispersion, analytical solutions, finite difference and finite element methods, advection-dominated problems, inverse modelling, and applications such as seawater intrusion and quality management.

Best for: Numerical methods and analytical solutions for simulating contaminant transport in groundwater.
