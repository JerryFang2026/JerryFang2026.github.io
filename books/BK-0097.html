Water Well Drilling — WITH CABLE TOOLS

A 1958 manual on drilling water wells with cable tools. Covers history, geology, well purposes and types. Includes extensive tables on tool joints, rope sockets, cable requirements, water flow measurement, weirs, and screen diameters.

Best for: Cable tool drilling techniques, equipment specifications, and flow measurement tables.
