Reading context

The core concepts of unit hydrograph analysis, hydrograph separation, flood routing and Penman evaporation remain conceptually sound, but the book predates the Flood Studies Report (1975) and Flood Estimation Handbook (1999) and uses 1960s UK methods and data. Do not rely on this for current UK design rainfall, flood frequency, reservoir safety or regulatory standards; check all methods and parameters against later editions or modern FEH practice.

For the same author's updated treatment use Wilson, Engineering Hydrology, 4th ed. (1990); for current UK practice use Shaw, Beven, Chappell and Lamb, Hydrology in Practice, 4th ed. (2011), which covers Flood Estimation Handbook methods.
