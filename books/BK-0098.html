Water-Well Design and Construction

Textbook covering hydrologic cycle, groundwater occurrence, well hydraulics, Darcy's law, aquifer properties, radial-flow equations, gravel packs, well construction and development, aquifer tests, packer tests, and groundwater monitoring.

Best for: Principles of well design, hydraulics, construction, development, and aquifer testing methods.
