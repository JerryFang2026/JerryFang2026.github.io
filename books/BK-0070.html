Chemistry

Covers foundational general chemistry: atomic structure, stoichiometry, mass relationships, reactions in aqueous solution, gases, thermochemistry, quantum theory, periodic trends, and chemical bonding, with worked examples and applied 'Chemistry in Action' boxes on biological and environmental topics.

Best for: refreshing core general chemistry, especially aqueous reactions, redox, and bonding, before moving into water-quality and hydrogeochemical modelling

Core chemical laws and concepts remain valid, but this 2005 edition pre-dates later IUPAC updates and the current 15th edition (2024). Do not rely on this for current IUPAC-recommended atomic weights, chemical nomenclature, or regulatory water-quality standards; check against a modern edition or open text.
