Corrosion and Incrustation of Water Wells — PREVENTION AND REHABILITATION

Anthology of articles on well corrosion, incrustation, and rehabilitation. Topics include well development, pitting, sand problems, iron bacteria control, and chemical or physical treatment methods.

Best for: Diagnosing and treating well corrosion, incrustation, and iron bacteria problems.
