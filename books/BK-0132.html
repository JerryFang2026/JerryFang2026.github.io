Groundwater Recharge — A Guide to Understanding and Estimating Natural Recharge

Covers methods for estimating natural groundwater recharge. Sampled headings include interaquifer flows, recharge from irrigation, canal loss measurement, and model-based estimation in various aquifer settings.

Best for: Recharge estimation techniques, irrigation losses, and comparative groundwater model applications.
