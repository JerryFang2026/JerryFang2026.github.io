A Manual of Field Hydrogeology

Covers field hydrogeology from site reconnaissance, surface-water and groundwater measurements, aquifer and aquitard testing, soil/rock description, drilling and sampling, well design and installation, geochemical measurements, hydrogeologic mapping, and applied field projects.

Best for: practical field procedures for hydrogeological site investigations: measuring water levels and surface water, describing soils and rocks, overseeing drilling and well installation, sampling groundwater, and constructing water-level maps and flow nets.

Reading context

The core field procedures—soil and rock description, borehole drilling, well installation, water-level measurement, and flow-net construction—remain valid. Instrumentation, low-flow sampling protocols, and QA/QC practice have advanced; do not rely on this for current regulatory sampling guidance or modern datalogger/telemetry methods.

No later edition; use Weight, Practical Hydrogeology: Principles and Field Applications, 3rd ed. (McGraw Hill, 2019) as the modern field companion.
