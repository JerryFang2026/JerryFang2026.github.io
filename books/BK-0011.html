Reading context

High risk: predates the 1989 privatisation, the 1991 legislation, the Environment Agency, modern WRMPs and climate adaptation frameworks. Institutional responsibilities and procedures must not be carried over directly.

For real planning work, consult current materials such as the Environment Agency National Framework for Water Resources 2025.
