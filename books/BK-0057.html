Aquatic Chemistry Concepts

Presents the thermodynamic foundations of aquatic chemistry and acid–base equilibria, from activity coefficients and standard states through proton/electroneutrality/mass-balance equations, quantitative speciation calculations, pH as a master variable, titrations, buffer intensity and dissolved CO₂ chemistry.

Best for: building a rigorous understanding of aquatic acid–base equilibria, speciation calculations and graphical pH methods
