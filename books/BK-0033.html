Hydrogeology — Principles and Practice

Covers physical hydrogeology from porosity and Darcy's law to regional flow systems, chemical and isotope hydrogeology, coupling of groundwater and geological processes, and catchment-scale recharge, stream gauging, surface water–groundwater interaction, with introductory management frameworks.

Best for: undergraduate and postgraduate hydrogeology students needing an integrated treatment of physical, chemical and isotope hydrogeology with UK and international examples

Reading context

Core physical and chemical principles remain sound, and field and laboratory methods are still taught from this edition. However, it has been superseded by the third edition (2021), which adds over 350 new references and updated material on global groundwater fluxes, climate change and contaminant hydrogeology. Do not rely on this for current regulatory requirements or recent contaminant transport advances.

Use the substantially revised third edition, Hiscock & Bense, Hydrogeology: Principles and Practice, 3rd edition (Wiley-Blackwell, 2021, ISBN 978-1-119-56953-4), as the current replacement.
