Geology and Water — An introduction to fluid mechanics for geologists

Introduces fluid mechanics for geologists, covering hydrostatics, buoyancy and effective stress; Bernoulli flow and Stokes' law; Darcy's law and permeability limits; aquifer flow, qanats and artesian systems; compaction, abnormal pore pressures, basin deformation and sliding; and capillary immiscible flow.

Best for: geologists and groundwater professionals seeking a concise introduction to subsurface fluid pressures and Darcy-scale flow in sedimentary basins

Reading context

The fundamental derivations for hydrostatics, Bernoulli flow, Darcy's law and effective stress remain valid, but the book predates numerical modelling and modern field methods. Do not rely on this for regulatory hydrogeology, contaminant transport, groundwater dating or transient pumping-test analysis; use current standards and modelling tools.

No revised later edition exists (the 2011 softcover and 2012 eBook are unrevised reprints); use Ingebritsen, Sanford & Neuzil, Groundwater in Geologic Processes, 2nd ed., Cambridge University Press, 2006, as the modern replacement.
