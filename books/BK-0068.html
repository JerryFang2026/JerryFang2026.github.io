Geohydrology

A 1965 mathematical treatment of the hydrologic cycle, subsurface water, Darcy's law, steady-state groundwater flow, and leaky-aquifer functions, with numerical and experimental methods including Hele-Shaw models, resistance-capacity networks, and conductive-liquid/solid analogues.

Best for: classic mathematical derivations of Darcy's law, steady-state flow nets, and analogue/numerical methods (Hele-Shaw, R-C networks) in their original 1965 treatment

Reading context

The core flow theory (Darcy's law, steady-state flow nets, basic aquifer hydraulics) remains valid, but notation and non-SI units are obsolete. Do not rely on this for contaminant hydrogeology, numerical modelling, or modern well-test interpretation; check against current texts and software.

No later edition exists; use Fetter & Kreamer, Applied Hydrogeology, 5th ed. (Waveland Press, 9781478646525) as the modern replacement, with the free full text of Freeze & Cherry, Groundwater (1979), at fc79.gw-project.org for foundational theory.
