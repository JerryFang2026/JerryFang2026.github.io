Proceedings of the Groundwater Recharge Conference 1980 — held at James Cook University, Townsville, Queensland, 14-18 July 1980, under the auspices of the AWRC Technical Committee on Underground Water

Proceedings of a 1980 AWRC conference covering problems in estimating groundwater recharge, natural recharge evaluations for Anna Bay, Herbert Delta and Western Port Basin, natural isotope methods, recharge using treated sewage, and mathematical modelling of natural and artificial recharge.

Best for: historical Australian recharge estimation case studies and early artificial-recharge methods

Reading context

The regional field data remain useful as historical baselines, but the reported recharge rates and estimation methods must be checked against Crosbie et al. (2010) and Healy (2010). Do not rely on this for current Australian recharge values, modern tracer and chloride-mass-balance methods, or managed-aquifer-recharge design and regulation.

No later edition; for current Australian recharge estimates use Crosbie et al. (2010) and for estimation methodology use Healy (2010).
