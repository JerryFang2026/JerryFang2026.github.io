Hydrology and the River Environment

Covers the global and catchment water balance, precipitation, streamflow, runoff processes, floods and droughts, then freshwater habitats, river and wetland systems, and predictive models, with principles of river management and environmental protection.

Best for: a process-based overview of catchment hydrology and freshwater habitats for environmental science and geography

Reading context

The process hydrology and habitat concepts remain valid, but the regulatory, monitoring-technology and flood/drought material pre-dates the EU Water Framework Directive, natural flood management, and modern hydrometric practice. Do not rely on this for current river-basin planning, flood estimation methods, or ecological status assessment.

No later edition; use Newson's Land, Water and Development: Sustainable and Adaptive Management of Rivers (3rd ed., Routledge, 2009) as the modern companion.
