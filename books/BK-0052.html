Hydrology — An Introduction to Hydrologic Science

Graduate-level introduction to hydrologic science covering solar and atmospheric controls on precipitation, evaporation and transpiration, snowmelt, unsaturated-zone flow and infiltration, hydrograph and unit-hydrograph analysis, flood routing, probabilistic methods, and fluvial geomorphology.

Best for: readers seeking a rigorous, equation-driven treatment of classical physical hydrology and rainfall–runoff analysis

Reading context

The core physical principles and analytical methods (radiation and energy balance, Penman evaporation, unsaturated flow, unit hydrographs, Muskingum and St Venant routing, probability distributions) remain valid. Do not rely on this for remote-sensing precipitation or evapotranspiration, land-surface/climate coupling, modern statistical hydrology software, or current datasets and standards.

No later edition; for a current graduate-level treatment use Brutsaert, Hydrology: An Introduction, 2nd ed. (Cambridge University Press, 2023), ISBN 978-1-107-13527-7.
