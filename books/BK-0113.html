Hand Drilled Wells — A Manual on Siting, Design, Construction and Maintenance

Manual on siting, design, construction, and maintenance of hand-drilled wells. Covers groundwater basics, filter pipe and gravel pack installation, and casing removal methods.

Best for: Practical techniques for manual well drilling, completion, and maintenance in low-resource settings.
