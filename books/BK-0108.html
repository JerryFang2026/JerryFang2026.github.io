Evaluation and Control of Corrosion and Encrustation In Tube Wells of the Indus Plains, West Pakistan

Examines corrosion and encrustation in tube wells in the Indus Plains. Covers hydrologic setting, water-quality characteristics, and includes stability diagrams and photographs of encrusted well components.

Best for: Case studies and water-chemistry factors affecting well corrosion and encrustation.
