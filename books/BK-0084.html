Design of Water-Resource Systems — New Techniques for Relating Economic Objectives, Engineering Analysis, and Governmental Planning

Explores methods for designing multi-unit, multipurpose water-resource systems. Discusses simulation, mathematical models, and synthesizing streamflow sequences for river basin analysis, linking economic objectives with engineering analysis and governmental planning.

Best for: System design methods, simulation techniques, and mathematical models for river basins.
