Geosciences in Development — Proceedings of an International Conference on the Application of the Geosciences in Developing Countries / Nottingham / 26-29 September 1988

Proceedings from a 1988 conference on applying geosciences in developing countries. Visible sections cover aid policies, geoscience education, hydrogeology, and economic minerals. Includes papers on phosphate resources, placers, small-scale mining, and exploration techniques.

Best for: Conference papers on geoscience applications, education, and mineral resources in developing nations.
