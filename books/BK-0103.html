Significance of Ground-Water Chemistry in Performance of North Sahara Tube Wells in Algeria and Tunisia

A U.S. Geological Survey water-supply paper examining how groundwater chemistry affects tube well performance in North Sahara. Includes field tests, corrosion deposits, and water chemistry diagrams.

Best for: Well corrosion, water chemistry stability fields, and salinized soil observations.

Reading context

The results describe the studied North Sahara wells in their historical setting. They are not current water-quality measurements or present-day operating limits.
