Iron Bacteria Occurrence — Problems and Control Methods in Water Wells

Reviews the taxonomy, morphology, physiology and environmental conditions of iron bacteria in water wells, then examines physical clogging and bacteriologically assisted corrosion, and evaluates chemical and physical control methods including disinfectants, acids, surfactants, UV light and pasteurisation.

Best for: a concise 1985 literature review of iron-bacteria taxonomy, clogging mechanisms and chemical/physical control options for wells

Reading context

The basic occurrence and clogging/corrosion mechanisms it describes remain valid, but the chemical dosing, diagnostic methods and rehabilitation practice are dated. Do not rely on this for current biofilm/BART-based diagnosis, rehabilitation chemistry, or well-maintenance standards; check Cullimore (2008), Smith & Comeskey (2010) and current NGWA guidance.

No later edition; for current practice use Cullimore (2008) Practical Manual of Groundwater Microbiology, Smith & Comeskey (2010) Sustainable Wells, and the free NGWA Iron Bacteria information sheet.
