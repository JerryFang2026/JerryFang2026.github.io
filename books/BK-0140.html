Advanced Simulation and Modelling for Urban Groundwater Management — UGROW

Manual for the UGROW urban groundwater model. Covers interface tools, terrain mapping, numerical grids, hydraulic conductivity, water level calibration, flow paths, and capture zones in case studies.

Best for: UGROW software operation, model setup, and urban groundwater simulation examples.
