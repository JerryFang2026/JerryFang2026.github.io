Hydrology and Hydrochemistry of British Wetlands

An edited volume on the hydrology and water chemistry of British wetlands. Sections cover runoff generation, solute processes in peat, nitrogen and phosphorus dynamics, water colour management, and wetland re-creation.

Best for: Wetland hydrological processes, peatland chemistry, and nutrient dynamics.
