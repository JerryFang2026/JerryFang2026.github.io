The Geology of Libya — Volume V — Third Symposium on the Geology of Libya, held at Tripoli, September 27–30, 1987

Symposium papers on Libyan sedimentation and sedimentary petrology. Covers Palaeozoic deltaic sedimentation in Al Kufrah Basin, Silurian-Devonian series in Ghadāmis Basin, Nubian Sandstone trace elements, and Murzuq Basin stratigraphy.

Best for: Sedimentology and stratigraphy of Libyan basins.
