Gambling With Groundwater—Physical, Chemical, and Biological Aspects of Aquifer-Stream Relations — Physical, Chemical, and Biological Aspects of Aquifer-Stream Relations

Proceedings from the 1998 joint IAH-AIH congress covering stream-groundwater interaction, arid-zone resources, drugs in groundwater, karst contamination, tunnel hydraulics, and national groundwater protection schemes. Includes case studies from multiple countries.

Best for: Aquifer-stream interaction, groundwater protection risk assessment, and international case studies.
