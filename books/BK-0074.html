Principles of Metallic Corrosion

A concise teaching monograph covering oxidation of metals and alloys in gaseous environments, electrochemical corrosion in neutral, acid and alkaline aqueous solutions, atmospheric corrosion, and prevention and control methods including protective coatings, cathodic protection and non-metallic materials, plus stress and inter-granular corrosion phenomena.

Best for: hydrogeologists and groundwater engineers needing the electrochemical and environmental fundamentals of metallic corrosion

Reading context

The electrochemical fundamentals (oxidation, aqueous corrosion, polarisation, passivity, galvanic effects, cathodic protection principles) remain valid as an introductory treatment. Do not rely on this for current materials selection, coating specifications, cathodic protection design standards, or well-component corrosion decisions; consult NPL guides and a modern corrosion engineering text.

No later edition; use Jones, Principles and Prevention of Corrosion (2nd ed., Pearson) as the authoritative modern replacement, the open NPL Guides to Good Practice in Corrosion Control (Nos. 5 and 9) for practical guidance on bimetallic and steel corrosion, and Misstear, Banks & Clark (2017) Water Wells and Boreholes (2nd ed.) for well-casing/screen corrosion specifically.
