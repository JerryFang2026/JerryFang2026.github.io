Geology and Radwaste

A systematic treatment of radioactive waste sources, disposal concepts, geological time, weathering, sorption and radionuclide migration, host rock types, faulting and seismicity, fluid-rock interaction, natural analogues, glaciation and climate, and site selection.

Best for: understanding geological barriers, natural analogues, host rock selection and early safety thinking on geological disposal

The underlying geoscience remains valid; waste inventories, barrier design, THMC models, safety cases, public participation and regulatory regimes must follow modern standards.

For real siting questions, consult IAEA and current national geological disposal safety-case and regulatory materials.
