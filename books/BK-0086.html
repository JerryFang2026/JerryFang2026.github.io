Groundwater Transport: Handbook of Mathematical Models — Handbook of Mathematical Models

A handbook detailing mathematical models for groundwater contaminant transport. Covers problem statements, sources of contamination, numerical methods, matrix solvers, computer codes, and a discussion on method choice and data needs.

Best for: Mathematical models and numerical methods for contaminant transport.
