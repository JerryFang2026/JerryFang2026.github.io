Corrosion Engineering

Covers corrosion cost, electrochemical principles, polarisation and passivity, the eight forms of corrosion (including pitting, crevice, stress corrosion and hydrogen damage), test methods from exposure trials to electrochemical techniques, and corrosion behaviour of metals, alloys, plastics and elastomers.

Best for: a structured introduction to corrosion mechanisms, laboratory test methods, and material selection for metals and nonmetallics

Reading context

The electrochemical principles and the classification of eight forms of corrosion remain valid and widely taught. Do not rely on this for current NACE/AMPP standards, modern alloy grades, coatings or cathodic-protection practice; material data and cost figures date from 1986.

No later edition; use Roberge, Handbook of Corrosion Engineering (2nd ed., 2012) or Revie & Uhlig, Corrosion and Corrosion Control (4th ed., 2008) for modern data and standards.
