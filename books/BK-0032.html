Reading context

The baseline-geochemistry concepts and aquifer case studies remain the standard reference, but the policy chapter reflects the pre-2009 EU Groundwater Directive framework and earlier WFD interpretation. Do not rely on this for current regulatory threshold values, monitoring-cycle requirements or post-Brexit UK groundwater standards; check current EU and UK agency guidance.

No later edition; the open BGS/Environment Agency baseline reports for England and Wales provide the UK companion data, and current EU/UK groundwater directive guidance supersedes the policy chapter.
