Surface and Subsurface Mapping in Hydrogeology

A practical manual covering the preparation of hydrogeological field surveys; the construction, symbolisation, and interpretation of hydrogeological maps and cross-sections; remote sensing and geophysical prospecting; exploratory drilling and borehole geophysics; and groundwater-quality mapping.

Best for: hydrogeologists needing a systematic, pre-GIS treatment of field survey planning, hydrogeological map and cross-section construction, and airphoto/geophysical interpretation

Reading context

The underlying principles of hydrogeological field survey design, map and cross-section types, and qualitative interpretation of remote sensing and geophysical anomalies remain instructive. However, its manual contouring, cross-section and map-construction workflows pre-date GIS, digital elevation models, 3D geological modelling and the IAH/UNESCO standard map legend; do not rely on this for current map symbology, digital data handling or modern geophysical processing/interpretation software.

No later edition; for current hydrogeological mapping standards use Struckmeier & Margat (1995) Hydrogeological Maps: A Guide and a Standard Legend, freely available from IAH, and pair it with modern GIS/3D geological modelling and geophysics texts.
