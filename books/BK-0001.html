Pollution in the U.K.

A compact overview of UK pollution-control law as it stood in the mid-1990s, covering common law, regulatory bodies, environmental protection legislation, waste, water pollution, environmental auditing and the EC rules of the day.

Best for: tracing UK environmental-law concepts, superseded legislation and institutional history; checking the 1990s legal context

Reading context

High risk: Brexit, devolution, the environmental permitting regime and the Environment Act 2021 have changed the current framework. Do not rely on this to determine permits, liability, limits or procedures.

For real-world questions, first establish the jurisdiction (England/Wales/Scotland/Northern Ireland), then consult current government legislation and permitting guidance.
