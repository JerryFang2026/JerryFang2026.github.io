Principles of Petroleum Prospecting

Covers petroleum geology, reservoir porosity/permeability, traps and cap-rock sealing, primary and secondary hydrocarbon migration, maturation and organic-matter richness, Arabian Gulf case studies, and Monte Carlo computer applications for reserve and resource evaluation, with worked problems.

Best for: researchers wanting Magara's compaction, abnormal-pressure and fluid-migration concepts, capillary and pressure seals, and early Monte Carlo petroleum reserve estimation with Arabian Gulf examples

Reading context

The core reservoir, trap, capillary-pressure and compaction-fluid-migration concepts remain a valid conceptual introduction, but the exploration workflow, basin modelling and reserve-evaluation methods are outdated. Do not rely on this for current prospect risking, petroleum-systems modelling, 3D seismic interpretation or modern reserve/resource estimation.

No later edition; use Bjorlykke (ed.), Petroleum Geoscience: From Sedimentary Environments to Rock Physics, 2nd ed., Springer, 2015 as the modern replacement.
