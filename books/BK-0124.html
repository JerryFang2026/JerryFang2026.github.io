Geoecology of the Nigerian part of the Lake Chad Basin

Study of the Nigerian Lake Chad Basin covering regional setting, economy, drought in Borno, hydrology and hydrogeology. Examines the Ngadda River system, aquifers, hydrological balance, and deep geological impacts on surface runoff.

Best for: Lake Chad Basin hydrology, aquifers, drought impacts, and surface runoff relationships.
