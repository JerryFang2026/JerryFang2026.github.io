Physical Processes of Sedimentation — An Introduction

Introduces the physical processes governing sediment erosion, transport and deposition, from fluid mechanics and boundary layers to sedimentary structures, then applies them to aeolian, fluvial, shallow-marine, turbidity-current and glacial environments.

Best for: a concise introduction to the physical basis of sediment transport and the origin of primary sedimentary structures in clastic environments

Reading context

The fluid-mechanics fundamentals, dimensional reasoning and descriptions of structures remain valid, but bedform stability, turbulence measurements and process-based facies models have advanced considerably since 1970. Do not rely on this for modern sediment-transport formulae, sequence-stratigraphic interpretation or field standards.

The direct modern successor is Allen's Principles of Physical Sedimentology (Allen & Unwin, 1985; Blackburn Press reprint in print; Springer eBook DOI 10.1007/978-94-010-9683-6).
