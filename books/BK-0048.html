Hydrometeorology

This 1970 monograph covers the principles of hydrometeorology, including atmospheric processes and water balance, measurement and analysis of precipitation, storm models, and statistical estimation of probable maximum precipitation for engineering design.

Best for: historical hydrometeorological principles and pre-WMO storm-model / PMP procedures

Reading context

The atmospheric-physics and water-balance basics remain sound, but its storm-model and statistical PMP procedures have been superseded by the WMO Manual on Estimation of Probable Maximum Precipitation (WMO-No. 1045, 3rd ed. 2009) and by climate-change-aware extreme-rainfall methods. Do not rely on this for current regulatory PMP estimates or modern extreme-rainfall frequency analysis.

No later edition; use the WMO Manual on Estimation of Probable Maximum Precipitation (PMP), WMO-No. 1045, 3rd ed. 2009 (open full text) as the authoritative modern replacement for its PMP and storm-model chapters.
