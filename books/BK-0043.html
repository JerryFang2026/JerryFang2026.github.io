Proceedings of the International Groundwater Conference on Sustainable Development and Management of Groundwater Resources in Semi-Arid Region with Special Reference to Hard Rocks — IGC-2002, Dindigul, Tamil Nadu, India, February 20-22, 2002

Papers from IGC-2002 cover groundwater potential assessment using GIS, geophysics and remote sensing; recharge through natural and artificial processes; pollution and water quality; and aquifer characterisation of fractured and weathered hard-rock systems, with Indian, African and Sri Lankan case studies.

Reading context

The hydrogeological case studies remain useful as field examples, but the GIS, remote-sensing and numerical modelling methods are dated, and all regulatory and standards content must be checked against current Indian, UK and international sources. Do not rely on this for current software workflows or regulatory compliance.

No later edition; the same proceedings remain in print from Routledge/CRC as ISBN 978-90-5809-263-2, with a later synthesis in Groundwater Dynamics in Hard Rock Aquifers: Sustainable Management and Optimal Monitoring Network Design (Springer).
