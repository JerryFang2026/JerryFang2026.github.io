Ames Irrigation Handbook — For Irrigation Engineers

This manufacturer’s handbook covers farm resource inventory, gross irrigation amount and frequency computations, sprinkler system layouts (including gated pipe), piping-system hydraulics for laterals and main lines, water hammer and air-valve sizing, Lo-Hi pipe capacity tables, and profit-planning cost factors.

Best for: historical W. R. Ames sprinkler-system layout and piping-hydraulics charts, especially Lo-Hi pipe and profit-planning cost tables

Reading context

The general hydraulic principles and design sequence remain useful conceptually, but the product-specific Lo-Hi pipe and coupler data, US customary design charts and 1960s profit-planning costs are obsolete. Do not rely on this for current sprinkler system design, regulatory compliance or life-cycle cost analysis.

Use Keller & Bliesner, Sprinkle and Trickle Irrigation (1990; Blackburn Press reprint) as the authoritative successor, and the USDA-NRCS National Engineering Handbook Part 623, Chapter 11 'Sprinkler Irrigation' (2016) as the open current standard.
