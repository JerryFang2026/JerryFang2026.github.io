Groundwater in the Urban Environment — Selected City Profiles

Case studies on urban groundwater issues in developing cities. Includes profiles of Dhaka, Tangshan, Belgrade, Haukipudas, and the Urals covering exploitation, contamination, geotechnical problems, and waste disposal impacts.

Best for: City-specific urban groundwater case studies and contamination examples.
