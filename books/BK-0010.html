The Physics of Flow Through Porous Media

An in-depth treatment of pore-space structure, Darcy's law, permeability, non-Darcy flow, statistical theory, and miscible and immiscible multiphase displacement, spanning groundwater, petroleum and soil mechanics.

Best for: understanding permeability, capillarity, flow equations, multiphase displacement and classic theoretical derivations

Reading context

Modern pore-scale imaging, digital rock physics, computational multiphase flow, upscaling, reactive transport and uncertainty analysis are not covered.

Suitable as a source for mechanisms and classic derivations; not to be used on its own as a modern numerical modelling handbook.
