Groundwater

The classic textbook that places groundwater flow, regional flow systems, hydrochemistry, well hydraulics, contaminant transport, resource evaluation and geotechnical engineering within a single unified framework.

Best for: foundational concepts: Darcy flow, regional groundwater flow, advection-dispersion, natural hydrochemical evolution and contaminant hydrogeology
