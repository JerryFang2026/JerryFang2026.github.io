Hydrogeology of the Dinaric Karst

Examines the geology and hydrogeology of the Dinaric karst region in Yugoslavia. Contents cover karst terrain theory, crustal structure, regional classification, speleological phenomena, and karst poljes.

Best for: Regional karst geology, speleology, and hydrogeologic classification of the Dinaric area.
