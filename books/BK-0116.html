Selection and Installation of Well Screens and Gravel Packs

An anthology of technical papers covering well design criteria, fundamental concepts, and procedures for gravel packing wells. Focuses on the selection and installation of screens and formation stabilizers.

Best for: Design criteria and installation procedures for well screens and gravel pack operations.
