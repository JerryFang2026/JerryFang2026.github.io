Sixth Salt Water Intrusion Meeting in Hanover 1979 — Geologisches Jahrbuch, Reihe C: Hydrogeologie, Ingenieurgeologie, Heft 29 (proceedings of the 6th SWIM, Hannover, opened 15 October 1979)

Proceedings of the 6th Salt Water Intrusion Meeting (Hannover, 1979) with regional case studies from Italian, English, Indian, Belgian and German coastal aquifers, plus analytical and field methods for interface location and salinisation processes, and special papers on submarine discharge and geophysical mapping.

Best for: historical regional saltwater-intrusion case studies and early analytical and geophysical interface methods presented at SWIM 1979

Reading context

The case studies and simple analytical and geophysical methods remain historically and instructionally valuable, but seawater-intrusion practice now depends on variable-density numerical modelling and modern monitoring. Do not rely on this for current management decisions, regulatory compliance, or up-to-date hydrochemical and geophysical standards.

No later edition; the complete scanned volume is open access via swim-site.nl, and Werner et al. (2013) in Advances in Water Resources is the authoritative modern review to use alongside later SWIM proceedings.
