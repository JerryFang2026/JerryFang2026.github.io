Groundwater Resources Modelling — A Case Study from the UK

Edited Geological Society case-study volume documenting the Environment Agency's national groundwater modelling programme for England and Wales, covering technical standards, stakeholder engagement, recharge estimation, regional Chalk, Permo-Triassic Sandstone, Carboniferous Limestone and alluvial aquifer models, model linking, abstraction impacts and uncertainty.

Best for: the Environment Agency national groundwater modelling programme and UK aquifer case studies, especially Chalk, Permo-Triassic Sandstone and Carboniferous Limestone

Reading context

The UK aquifer conceptual models and regulator-led programme history remain directly useful, but modelling codes, uncertainty methods and the post-2012 regulatory context (Water Framework Directive delivery, post-Brexit regulations) have moved on. Do not rely on this for current UK abstraction licensing requirements, numerical code defaults or modern uncertainty quantification; check current Environment Agency guidance and software documentation.

No later edition; pair it with the Environment Agency R&D Technical Report W214 framework and current Environment Agency groundwater modelling guidance as the modern companion.
