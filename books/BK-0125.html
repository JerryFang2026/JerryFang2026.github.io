BK-0125 · THE G15 BOOKSHELF

Modelling of biogeochemical processes and spatial patterns in freshwater wetlands — Modellering van biogeochemische processen en ruimtelijke patronen in vochtige oecosystemen (met een samenvatting in het Nederlands)

Doctoral thesis on modelling carbon, nitrogen, and phosphorus dynamics in river marginal wetlands. Uses dynamic simulation to examine landscape geochemical flows, nutrient cycling, and fertilisation effects on wetland biogeochemistry.

Best for: Wetland nutrient cycling models, carbon-nitrogen-phosphorus dynamics, and fertilisation simulation.

Reading context

This doctoral thesis presents research models rather than generally validated management tools. Parameter choices and applicability need to be assessed for a new wetland.
