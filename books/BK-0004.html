Construction Dewatering — A Guide to Theory and Practice

A systematic handbook of construction dewatering: excavation dewatering, pumping tests, wellpoints and deep wells, open sump pumping, ejector wells, cut-off walls, ground freezing, grouting, recharge, pumps, piping and electrics, costing and contracts.

Best for: comparing construction dewatering options, conventional system design, wellpoint/deep-well/cut-off methods and construction management
