Mathematics for the Physical Sciences

A Dover reprint of a 1962 mathematics text covering vector spaces, matrices, orthogonal functions, and ordinary differential equations. Includes chapters on numerical solutions, linear programming via the simplex algorithm, and polynomial approximation, with exercises and solutions.

Best for: Mathematical methods for physical science problems, including linear algebra and differential equations.
